# FinGrow

## Vision

FinGrow is a Personal Financial Growth Platform designed to help people build wealth through intentional spending, disciplined saving, and long-term financial planning.

Most people know how much they earn but do not know:

- Where their money goes
- Whether they are overspending
- Whether they are achieving financial goals
- How small financial decisions impact long-term wealth

FinGrow aims to solve this problem by providing a structured financial system that helps users allocate income, track expenses, monitor goals, and visualize their financial future.

The platform evolves beyond traditional expense tracking and focuses on helping users make better financial decisions.

---

## Core Principles

### Income First

Every financial plan starts with income.

Users allocate income into purpose-based categories before spending occurs.

### Goal-Based Spending

Every expense impacts future goals.

The platform helps users understand the relationship between spending and wealth creation.

### Financial Awareness

Users should always know:

- Current balances
- Spending patterns
- Goal progress
- Savings progress

### Long-Term Thinking

The platform promotes:

- Saving discipline
- Debt reduction
- Wealth creation
- Financial independence

---

## Long-Term Mission

Help users:

- Understand spending habits
- Build saving discipline
- Eliminate debt faster
- Understand the power of compounding
- Visualize long-term wealth growth
- Make informed financial decisions

The ultimate goal is to transform FinGrow into a personal financial operating system that empowers users to achieve financial freedom.
